With immediate effect, all warranty and alteration complaints related to Seat Covers, Accessories & Mats (SAM) will be handled directly by the AFAC Service Centre.

Complaint Process

  1. 1️⃣ Share complaint details. Share clear photos, complete complaint details and the required warranty documents on the contact details mentioned below.
  2. 2️⃣ Claim approval. The company will inspect the claim and approve it as either ✅ FOC (Free of Cost) or 💳 Chargeable.
  3. 3️⃣ Defective part. The Plant In-charge will confirm whether the defective part needs to be returned to the plant or scrapped locally.
  4. 4️⃣ Direct dispatch. After approval, the replacement or alteration material will be dispatched directly from the plant to your store.
  5. 5️⃣ Chargeable claims. For chargeable claims, payment can be made directly to the company through UPI or bank transfer. Material will be dispatched after the payment is received.

🚚 All courier and freight charges for approved warranty and alteration claims will be borne by the company.
