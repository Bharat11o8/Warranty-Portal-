With immediate effect, all warranty and alteration complaints related to Electronic Products will be handled directly by the AFAC Service Centre.

Simple warranty claim process

Every warranty claim must include the documents and photos listed below. Please send them together in a single message.

What every claim must include

  1. 1️⃣ Completed warranty card photo.
  2. 2️⃣ Warranty card serial number and product serial number together in one clear photo.
  3. 3️⃣ Clear photos showing the fault. If the fault is not visible, send a written description of the problem.
  4. 4️⃣ Invoice copy. The invoice is optional when the warranty card is available. If the warranty card is unavailable, the invoice is compulsory.

❌ Claims without the required documents and photos will not be accepted.

Important instructions

  • If the fault is found before installation, send the product exactly as it is.
  • Do not cut, open, break, repair or tamper with the product. Any product received in such condition will be rejected.
  • If the product can be repaired, the same product will be repaired and returned.
  • Replacement is not automatic and will be decided by AFAC.
  • Please refer to Annexure 1 below for the product categories covered under the “Repair First” category.

Claim settlement

✅ Approved claims will be settled through replacement of the same SKU only.

✅ If the approved SKU is out of stock, it will be supplied after restocking.

❌ Physical damage caused during or after installation is not covered under warranty.
